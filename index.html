About Us

The Bakerz Bite Story

Bakerz Bite started as a small neighbourhood oven and has grown into a reputable bakery and cafe, specialising in baked goods passionately made from the finest ingredients. Every day our bakers craft more than 300 varieties of artisan pastries, gourmet cakes, desserts and handcrafted beverages, fresh in-store.

We source carefully selected ingredients and promise freshness and quality in every bite. We use only real butter, real cream and unbleached flour, because we treat our customers as family. Our motto: Where smiles are served daily.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take custom cake orders?
Yes. Custom celebration cakes need at least 48 hours notice. Visit any branch or call us with your design idea and our cake artists will take care of the rest.
Are your products made fresh daily?
Absolutely. Everything on our shelves is baked fresh in-store every morning. Unsold fresh items are donated at closing time, never re-sold the next day.
Do you offer eggless or sugar-free options?
Yes, selected cakes, cookies and pastries are available in eggless and reduced-sugar versions. Ask our counter staff for today's fresh batch.
Can I place an order for home delivery?
Home delivery is available within the city on orders above Rs. 2,500. Delivery charges of Rs. 250 apply and cakes travel in insulated boxes.
What are your opening hours?
We are open every day from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including public holidays, because cravings do not take a day off.
